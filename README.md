This repository contains the complete solution, experimental evaluations, and codebase for the course **Synthetic Data Generation with Diffusion Models** (Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, AMU).

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective is to fix an acute class imbalance problem in fine-grained cat breed classification using synthetic data:
- **Baseline dataset**: 12 cat breeds, each with 70 training photos, except **Egyptian Mau** with only **15 training photos**.
- **Task**: Balance the minority class by adding 55 new samples (budget = 55) to reach 70 photos, comparing classical data augmentation against generative diffusion models across **5 random seeds** with an **EfficientNet-B0** classifier.

---

## 📊 Summary of Results

### 1. Meeting 1: Classical Real-Data Baselines
Compared the raw imbalanced dataset against exact oversampling and photometric/geometric augmentations:
- `m1-real`: Raw imbalanced data (15 Egyptian Mau photos).
- `m1-oversample`: Exact duplicate repetition of the 15 photos (`divmod` sampling).
- `m1-augment`: Classical transforms (RandomHorizontalFlip, RandomRotation 15°, ColorJitter 0.2, RandomAffine).
- `m1-augment-strong`: Aggressive transforms (RandomRotation 35°, heavy jitter 0.4, perspective distortion).

### 2. Meeting 2: Text-to-Image Generation (Diffusion Pools)
Generated 110 synthetic images per pool using diffusion models, trained classifiers across 5 seeds, and evaluated on unseen real validation and test sets:
- `m2-base`: Stable Diffusion 1.5 (20 steps, CFG guidance 7.5).
- `m2-guidance-2.5`: Low guidance scale (2.5 vs 7.5).
- `m2-steps-10`: Fast denoising (10 sampling steps).
- `m2-prompts-varied`: Multi-prompt cycling (close-ups, outdoor lawns, indoor sunlight).
- `m2-sdxl-turbo`: SDXL-Turbo distilled few-step model (4 steps).

## 💡 Key Scientific Takeaways

1. **Diffusion vs. Oversampling**:
   Synthetic data generated by diffusion models substantially outperformed simple oversampling (+14.0 pp validation recall: **0.800** vs **0.660**), resolving the background memorization bottleneck of repeating 15 photos.
2. **Sampling Steps (10 vs 20)**:
   Cutting sampling steps from 20 to 10 in SD 1.5 not only halved generation latency (~2.0s vs 3.8s per image) but also produced slightly softer, less over-sharpened images that generalized best to real cat photos (**0.800** validation recall).
3. **Prompt Diversity**:
   Varying prompt backgrounds and camera angles (`m2-prompts-varied`) achieved the lowest variance across random seeds (std = **0.022**), proving the value of linguistic scene diversification.
